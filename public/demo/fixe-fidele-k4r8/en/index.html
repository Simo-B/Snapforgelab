What's changing

The old phone network closes on 31 January 2027.

The PSTN is the old analogue phone network that runs over copper wires: the classic landline. ISDN is the older digital business line that uses the same network. Openreach is closing both, and Openreach and BT say there will be no extension.

Most firms: a digital line

Most businesses move to a digital line that runs over broadband (for example BT Digital Voice) and keep the same phone number.

Lines not moved in time

Lines that haven't been moved may be put on EVAc, a limited, temporary, last-resort service. It is not a long-term solution.

Your number

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Do I keep my number?

Yes. Your number stays with your current provider. We only receive the calls you forward to us.

What if I'm already on Digital Voice?

It works the same. Call forwarding works on a digital line just as it does on the old copper line.

Do I need new equipment?

No. There's nothing to buy or install. You keep your line and your phones, and the summaries arrive by text and email.

What about emergency calls?

999 calls are made from your own line as usual and never go through us. We only handle incoming calls you forward to us.

Does the caller know it's an AI?

Yes. The assistant says so at the start of every call.

What about data?

We follow UK GDPR. Caller details are used only to pass you the message, and are deleted on request.

What happens after the pilot?

You decide. There's no commitment: if you don't want to carry on, simply turn off call forwarding.
