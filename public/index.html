Replit app development FAQ

How much does a custom business app on Replit cost?

A Starter Build is a flat $2,500 for a defined app (dashboard, CRM, portal, automation or AI assistant). My next 3 founding clients pay $1,500 in exchange for a short case study. Larger scopes get a written fixed-price quote within 48 hours. No hourly billing.

How fast can my app be live?

Most Starter builds go live in about 7 days after I receive access to your data. Custom scopes come with a dated timeline before you pay anything.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The app lives on your own Replit account. It is standard code you can export, and it keeps running if you stop working with me.

Do I need a Replit account?

Yes, the app is deployed on your Replit account so you keep full ownership. I handle setup, deployment and configuration for you.

I already built an app with Replit Agent. Can you make it production-ready?

Yes. The Ship-Ready Review ($199) audits security, data, authentication, performance and deployment, and delivers a prioritized fix list within 48 hours. If I find fewer than 3 real issues, you get a full refund. Want me to fix everything? The Ship-It Sprint ($990) does it in 5 days, and the review fee is credited.

How do payments work?

The $49 Deploy Fix and the review are paid upfront via PayPal and covered by a refund guarantee. Sprints and builds are paid half at the start and half when your app is live in production. The first month of Care is included with every Sprint and Build; after that it is $249/month and you can cancel anytime.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Yes. I work fully async and remote with clients worldwide, in English, with written updates.
