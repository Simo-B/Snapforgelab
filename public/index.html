What I check

The security holes AI agents leave behind most often. The free audit looks at your live app from the outside, read-only, the way any visitor could.

Free security audit · from the outside

  • Secrets in the JS bundle: OpenAI, Stripe or Supabase service_role keys shipped to every visitor's browser.
  • Exposed Supabase data: signs that tables are reachable with the public anon key because Row Level Security is off.
  • Open CORS with credentials: any website able to call your API as your logged-in users.
  • Auth redirects to preview domains: login and reset links that send users to a dev or preview URL.
  • Transport and headers: HTTPS redirects, Content Security Policy, Stripe test keys left in production.

Safe to Launch · $149 · inside your code

  • Row Level Security, table by table: who can read and write what in your database.
  • Stripe webhooks: signature verification, so nobody can fake a "payment succeeded".
  • Access control: users opening other users' records by changing an ID.
  • Secrets and costs: keys in source, unbounded AI calls, no rate limits.
  • Data and deployment: backups, dev and prod sharing a database, error tracking.

Replit app development FAQ

Is the free security audit safe for my app?

Yes. The free audit is read-only: I only look at what your live app already shows any visitor (its pages, JavaScript files, headers and API responses). I never log in as your users, never submit forms, never change anything and never touch your data. If I find a leaked key, I tell you where it is, redacted, and never use it.

How much does a custom business app on Replit cost?

Tell me the workflow (dashboard, CRM, portal, automation or AI assistant) and you get a written fixed-price quote within 48 hours. No hourly billing.

How fast can my app be live?

Most Starter builds go live in about 7 days after I receive access to your data. Custom scopes come with a dated timeline before you pay anything.
