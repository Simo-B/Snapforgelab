FAQ

Replit app development FAQ

How much does a custom business app on Replit cost?

A Starter Build is a flat $2,500 for a defined app (dashboard, CRM, portal, automation or AI assistant). Our next 3 founding clients pay $1,500 in exchange for a short case study. Larger scopes get a written fixed-price quote within 48 hours. No hourly billing.

How fast can my app be live?

Most Starter builds go live in about 7 days after we receive access to your data. Custom scopes come with a dated timeline before you pay anything.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The app lives on your own Replit account. It is standard code you can export, and it keeps running if you stop working with us.

Do I need a Replit account?

Yes, the app is deployed on your Replit account so you keep full ownership. We handle setup, deployment and configuration for you.

I already built an app with Replit Agent. Can you make it production-ready?

Yes. The Ship-Ready Review ($199) audits security, data, authentication, performance and deployment, and delivers a prioritized fix list within 48 hours. If we find fewer than 3 real issues, you get a full refund. Want us to fix everything? The Ship-It Sprint ($990) does it in 5 days, and the review fee is credited.

How do payments work?

The review is paid upfront and covered by the refund guarantee. Sprints and builds are 50% to start and 50% when your app is live in production. The first month of Care is included with every Sprint and Build; after that it is $249/month and you can cancel anytime.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Yes. We work fully async and remote with teams worldwide, in English, with written updates.
