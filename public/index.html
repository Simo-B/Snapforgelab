FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a custom business app on Replit cost?

A Starter build is a flat $1,500 for a defined app (dashboard, CRM, portal, automation or AI assistant). Larger scopes get a written fixed-price quote within 48 hours. No hourly billing.

How fast can my app be live?

Most Starter builds go live in about 7 days after we receive access to your data. Custom scopes come with a dated timeline before you pay anything.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The app lives on your own Replit account. It is standard code you can export, and it keeps running if you stop working with us.

Do I need a Replit account?

Yes, the app is deployed on your Replit account so you keep full ownership. We handle setup, deployment and configuration for you.

I already built an app with Replit Agent. Can you make it production-ready?

Yes. The Ship-Ready Review ($290) audits security, data, authentication, performance and deployment, and delivers a prioritized fix list within 72 hours. The fee is credited if you then hire us to fix it.

How do payments work?

50% to start, 50% when your app is live in production. The monthly Run plan is optional and can be cancelled anytime.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Yes. We work fully async and remote with teams worldwide, in English, with written updates.
