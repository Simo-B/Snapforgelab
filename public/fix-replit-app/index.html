FAQ

How much does it cost to fix a Replit app?

It depends on what is broken. One deployment or configuration blocker costs $49 with the Deploy Fix, refunded if I can't fix it. A full security and production review is $199 at the launch price for the first 10 reviews, then $390. Fixing everything and putting the app in production is the $990 Ship-It Sprint, with the review fee credited. The first audit is free.

Can I fix my Replit app myself?

Often, yes. Each symptom above links to a free step-by-step guide. If the same bug survives a few attempts, or it touches logins, payments or customer data, get help before real users find it.

Do you fix apps built with Lovable or Bolt too?

Yes. The free audit and the $49 Deploy Fix also cover Lovable and Bolt apps. Start with the free Lovable and Bolt audit.

What if you can't fix it?

The Deploy Fix is refunded in full if the blocker isn't fixed within 48 hours of access. The review is refunded in full if I find fewer than 3 real issues.

Will you change things I didn't ask for?

Not during a Deploy Fix: it covers the blocker you describe, not new features, unrelated bugs or refactors. If I spot other problems along the way, I tell you, and you decide what to do about them.
