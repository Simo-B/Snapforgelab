Services and prices

  • Launch Fix Sprint ($990 flat): done-for-you fixes of the top issues found by the free audit (security, uptime, CORS and secrets, performance, SEO basics), on the list agreed in writing after a 15-minute call, within 5 business days of receiving access, with a retest and a written report. Paid in full by PayPal only after the fixes are verified working; nothing upfront.
  • Safe to Launch ($149; founding price $49 for the first 3 clients, in exchange for a testimonial): a security check of one AI-built app (Replit, Lovable, Bolt or Cursor) and the fixes, pushed and tested, within 48 hours of receiving access, with a 1-page report and a 5-minute video walkthrough. Paid upfront. Covers exposed keys and secrets, database access rules, login and accounts, payments and webhooks, deploy and environment settings, and backups.
  • Ship-It Sprint: implementation of an agreed fix list and deployment to production, on the scope agreed in writing.
  • Starter Build: one business app built on your Replit account, on the scope agreed in writing.
  • Care ($249/month, cancel anytime): continuous security monitoring of a live app, with a security re-check after every release plus a monthly full pass, an alert within 1 business day if a new issue appears, fixes for what the re-check finds, bug fixes and security updates, hosting and uptime monitoring, small monthly improvements, priority support within 1 business day. It stops at the end of the paid month.
  • Ship-Ready Prompt Pack: a PDF ($19, one-time) for your own use, emailed to your PayPal address within a few hours of payment. If it doesn't help, reply to the delivery email within 14 days for a full refund.

Prices are in US dollars and published on the Launch Fix Sprint and Safe to Launch pages. The price and scope confirmed to you in writing before you pay are the ones that apply.

Payment

Payments are made through PayPal. The Launch Fix Sprint is paid in full once the fixes are verified working, with nothing upfront. Safe to Launch is paid in full upfront. Ship-It Sprints (legacy) and Builds are paid 50% at the start and 50% when the app is live in production.

Refund guarantee

Launch Fix Sprint guarantee: you pay only once the fixes are verified working, and if a fixed issue comes back within 14 days, I fix it free. Safe to Launch guarantee: if a fixed issue comes back within 14 days, I fix it free. If I find nothing worth fixing, you pay nothing. For Ship-It Sprints and Builds, if we cannot deliver the agreed scope, you don't pay the second 50%.

Replit Deploy Fix

  • Scope: one specific deployment or configuration blocker that you describe: a failing build or publish, secrets or environment variables, a database connection, or a custom domain.
  • Access: you invite hello@snapforgelab.com to your Repl with edit access for the duration of the fix. We limit changes to what the fix needs.
  • What "fixed" means: the blocker you described no longer occurs and the app deploys or runs as it did before. New features, unrelated bugs and refactors are out of scope.
  • Refund: if we have not fixed the blocker within 48 hours of receiving access, you get a full refund. You are responsible for keeping a backup or checkpoint of your app before you grant access.
