Services and prices

  • Ship-Ready Review: a read-only production-readiness audit of one Replit app, delivered as a written report and a video within 48 hours of receiving access. Paid upfront.
  • Ship-It Sprint: implementation of the review's fix list and deployment to production, on the scope agreed in writing.
  • Starter Build: one business app built on your Replit account, on the scope agreed in writing.
  • Care: monthly hosting support, monitoring and improvements. Cancel anytime; it stops at the end of the paid month.

Prices are in US dollars and published on the pricing section. The price and scope confirmed to you in writing before you pay are the ones that apply.

Payment

Payments are made through PayPal. Reviews are paid in full upfront. Sprints and Builds are paid 50% at the start and 50% when the app is live in production. The review fee is credited toward a Ship-It Sprint booked within 30 days.

Refund guarantee

If a Ship-Ready Review finds fewer than 3 real issues, you get a full refund. For Sprints and Builds, if we cannot deliver the agreed scope, you don't pay the second 50%.

Timelines

Delivery times start when we receive the access and information we asked for. Delays caused by missing access, third-party outages or scope changes move the date accordingly; we tell you in writing.

Confidentiality

We keep your code, data and business information confidential and use them only to deliver the service. Case studies are published only with your written permission.

Responsibility

We work with care and to professional standards, but no review can guarantee that an app is free of every bug or vulnerability. You remain responsible for your app, your data, your users and your compliance obligations. Our total liability for any service is limited to the amount you paid for it.
