1. Prompt the fix yourself

Free if you already pay for Replit or Lovable. It works for a single, visible bug. It stops working when every prompt fixes one thing and breaks another, or when the problem is invisible: database rules, exposed keys, unverified payment webhooks. Rule of thumb: after three failed prompts on the same bug, stop and get a diagnosis. Why the Agent keeps breaking your app.

2. An hourly freelancer

Good when you can describe the task exactly. The risk with AI-built code is scope: the freelancer finds more than you expected and the clock keeps running. Ask for a fixed quote once they have read the code, not before.

How to choose

  • No users, one bug: prompt it yourself.
  • Live app taking signups or payments: an audit first (free to $500), then a fixed-price fix.
  • Needs a rebuild or a migration: an agency-style production pass.

Whoever you pick, ask three questions before you pay: Do you audit before you quote? Is the price fixed? What happens if the fix breaks again?

FAQ

How much does it cost to fix a Lovable or Replit app?

From $0 if you prompt the fix yourself, $49 to $500 for an audit, $149 to about $2,500 for typical fixes, and $6,000 or more for a full production pass, based on prices each provider published in October 2026.

Is my Lovable app safe to launch?

Not necessarily. Missing Row Level Security in apps generated by Lovable was published as CVE-2025-48757, rated critical (9.3). Lovable disputes it, saying each app owner is responsible for protecting their data. Check your database rules before real users arrive.

Should I rebuild my AI-built app from scratch?

Usually not. Most apps need a hardening pass, not a rewrite. A good audit tells you which, before you spend money on either.

Should I keep prompting the AI to fix it?

For one visible bug, yes. When each prompt fixes one thing and breaks another, or when the problem is invisible, such as database rules or exposed keys, more prompting usually makes it worse.
