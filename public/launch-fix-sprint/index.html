What I fix

  • Security: database access rules, users opening other users' data, admin routes, unverified payment webhooks.
  • CORS and secrets: keys shipped to the browser, open CORS with credentials, dev and prod mixed up.
  • Uptime: crashes, failed deploys, missing health checks and alerts.
  • Performance: slow first load, heavy bundles, slow queries on the pages that matter.
  • SEO basics: a title per page, real 404s, sitemap and robots.txt that work, social previews.
